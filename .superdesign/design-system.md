# Distrito Geek Design System

Distrito Geek is a dark-first curated catalog for RPG miniatures, action figures and collectibles. It sends buyers to official marketplace listings rather than operating checkout.

Use only the existing palette: near-black backgrounds, graphite surfaces, restrained borders, white text, muted gray supporting copy and yellow `#f4b900` as the primary accent. Shopee may use orange `#ff5d28`; success uses green `#20c975`. Preserve Inter/system body typography and condensed Arial Narrow/Impact display typography.

Cards are practical and product-led: real image, compact category label, readable title, synchronized price, availability and one clear primary navigation action. Secondary controls such as favorite and compare must remain small, accessible and visually subordinate.

New engagement surfaces should feel like utility layers, not a separate ecommerce checkout: favorite heart, compare checkbox/action, a bottom comparison tray, recently viewed rail and an empty-state favorites page. Use 10px radii, 1px borders, subtle shadow, 44px minimum interactive targets and strong visible focus. Support dark/light themes and mobile layouts from 320px.
